Privacy Policy

ApkaŁapka - mobile application for pet management

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Contact for all data matters: apkalapka.support@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to.

This policy explains what data we process, why, on what legal basis, who we share it with and what your rights are. It is for information only: using the App does not mean you consent to anything. Where we need your consent, we ask for it separately.

2. What data we process

2.1 Data you enter

  • Account data: email address and password (the password is stored securely by Firebase Authentication; we never see it). When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an account identifier, your email address and, if they provide it, your name and profile photo. Apple may provide a private relay address.
  • Pet data: name, species, breed, date of birth, weight, photos, visits, vaccinations, medications, deworming, test results, symptoms, feeding, hydration, litter box, grooming, activity, expenses, insurance, supplies and other entries.
  • Contacts: vet details and emergency numbers you enter. Only enter other people's details if you are entitled to do so.
  • Photos and attachments: photos from your camera or gallery and files attached to entries.
  • Reading text from photos: a photo of a test result or a food label is read by a text recognition engine (Google ML Kit) that runs only on your phone. Neither the photo nor the recognised text is sent to any server for this purpose.

2.2 Data generated while you use the App

  • Location: only when you open the clinic map and grant the system permission. Coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Overpass) to find nearby clinics. We do not store your location and never collect it in the background.
  • Food search: when you search for food by barcode or name, the barcode or name is sent to the Open Pet Food Facts database. When you paste a shop link, the App downloads that page directly from the shop. In both cases no data about you or your pet is sent.
  • Usage statistics (only with consent): Firebase Analytics records events such as “feature X opened” together with an installation identifier, device model, OS version and approximate country. We do not send the content of your entries, pet data or search queries. Without your consent statistics are switched off.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash information (stack trace, device model, OS and App version, installation identifier). This is pseudonymous data: it contains neither your email nor your entries.
  • App verification: Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity on Android, App Attest on iOS) checks that it is the genuine App connecting to our cloud. This protects your data from abuse.
  • Ads (free version only): Google AdMob shows ads and may use your device's advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent dialog asks for consent to personalised ads. On iOS the system also asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details. RevenueCat receives your App account identifier and your purchase history and status in order to unlock Premium.

2.3 The apkalapka.pl website

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it technically processes the visitor's IP address.
  • Site statistics: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without identifying individuals.
  • Fonts are served from our own server, not from Google.
  • Email: when you write to us, we process your address and message to reply.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Running the App, account, cloud sync, pet sharing, Premium, clinic map, food search: performance of the service contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Usage statistics and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the App Settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Crash reports, app verification, security, non-personalised ads in the free version: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in a stable and secure App and in funding the free version.
  • Replying to messages, handling complaints, establishing and defending claims: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Accounting and tax obligations related to sales: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Providing data is voluntary. You cannot create an account without an email address, but you can use the App in guest mode, in which case your data stays on your phone only.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects. Calculations in the App (e.g. calories, weight trends, test result ranges) are for information only.

4. Where data is stored

On your phone: the App database is encrypted (AES-256). Photos, attachments and automatic weekly backups are stored in the App's private folder, protected by the phone's operating system, but not additionally encrypted by the App. An export file you create yourself is not encrypted: keep it safe.

In the cloud: when you are signed in, pet data and photos are synced to Google Firebase (Firestore and Storage). Only you and the people you share a pet with can access them. Connections are encrypted (TLS).

5. Who we share data with

  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC): Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check, AdMob, ML Kit (runs on the phone), Google sign-in. Firebase privacy, Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store, App Tracking Transparency.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription management. RevenueCat privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation and Overpass servers: map and clinic search (coordinates only).
  • Open Pet Food Facts: food database (barcode or product name only).
  • GitHub, Inc. and Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting and statistics.
  • People you share a pet with (section 6).
  • Public authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell your data.

Transfers outside the EEA: Google, Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub and Cloudflare may process data in the USA. We rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for companies certified under it and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request a copy of these safeguards by writing to us.

6. Sharing a pet with other people

With Premium you can share a pet with another person who has an App account: by email, or with a QR code, link or code to type in. You choose a role:

  • Family and Sitter: view all of the pet's data (a sitter's access can have an expiry date).
  • Vet: view medical data only (visits, vaccinations, medications, test results, weight, deworming, meal plans, grooming).

The invited person sees your email address and the pet's name. Sharing gives view-only access, without editing. You can revoke access at any time. You are responsible for choosing whom to invite; the invited person is responsible for what they do with the data they have seen.

7. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it in the App, delete your account or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud data: until you delete your account. Account deletion immediately removes pet data, photos, invitations and shares from our cloud; Google's backups are cleared as part of their rotation cycle.
  • Usage statistics: no longer than 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase data at RevenueCat: until your account is deleted, or earlier at your request. Google and Apple also keep transaction history under their own rules. Data needed for tax settlements is kept for the period required by law.
  • Correspondence: up to 3 years after the matter is closed, because of possible claims.

8. Your rights

  • Access and a copy of your data: Settings → Backup → Export my data, or email us.
  • Rectification: edit any entry in the App.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete account, or via the account deletion page.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Data portability: JSON export.
  • Objection to processing based on our legitimate interest (e.g. crash reports), on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Withdrawal of consent to statistics and personalised ads: in the App Settings, at any time.
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority: in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl, or the authority in your EU country of residence.

Send requests to apkalapka.support@gmail.com. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account, e.g. by replying from the address it is registered to.

9. California residents (CCPA)

You have the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, to request its deletion and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information. Send requests to apkalapka.support@gmail.com.

10. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. People under 16 may use it only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has given us data without such consent, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use database encryption on the phone (AES-256), encrypted connections (TLS), cloud access rules limiting reads to the owner and the people they share a pet with, and app verification (App Check). No method is, however, 100% secure.

Data breaches: if a breach occurs that may affect you, we will report it to the Polish supervisory authority (UODO) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and, where it is likely to result in a high risk to your rights, inform you without undue delay (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

12. Changes to this policy

When the way we process data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. We will announce significant changes in the App in advance. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it separately.
